UNDERSTANDING HEAT PUMPS

How Does a Heat Pump Work?

A heat pump moves thermal energy from one location to another using a refrigeration cycle.

01

Energy Collection

The system absorbs thermal energy from the air, ground, or water.

02

Compression

A compressor increases the refrigerant's pressure and temperature.

03

Heat Transfer

The collected heat is transferred into the building's heating system.

04

Continuous Cycle

Refrigerant pressure is reduced and the cycle begins again.

01 / REFRIGERANT TECHNOLOGY

Refrigerant R454C

R454C is a refrigerant blend developed for selected refrigeration and heat pump applications. It has a lower global warming potential than several traditional refrigerants.

R454C is classified as an A2L refrigerant, meaning lower toxicity and mild flammability under the applicable classification. Its use requires compatible equipment and appropriate safety procedures.

Refrigerant selection must follow equipment specifications and applicable safety standards.
